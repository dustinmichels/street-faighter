# How to play

![A sweep knocks the opponent off their feet](/media/sweep.png){.sf-pixel}

## Starting a match

1. **PRESS START**: any key, a click, or a gamepad button.
2. **ENTER YOUR NAME**: up to 10 characters. <kbd>Enter</kbd> confirms, <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back. Your name appears under your health bar, and online your opponent sees it too.
3. Pick a mode:
   - **1 PLAYER VS CPU** opens **CHOOSE YOUR FIGHTER**. Pick the built-in fighter or one you [made yourself](./custom-characters), then **FIGHT!**. You play the fighter on the left.
   - **2 PLAYERS ONLINE** hosts or joins a match against another player. See [Online multiplayer](./online).
   - **CHARACTERS** is your fighter library: preview, import, and [create new fighters](./custom-characters).

A match starts with a **3, 2, 1, FIGHT!** countdown. Each fighter has 100 health; every punch, air kick, and sweep takes 10, so ten clean hits win. Press <kbd>R</kbd> to restart at any time against the CPU.

If both fighters use the same model, the computer's colors are hue-shifted so you can tell them apart.

## Controls

| Action      | Keyboard                                       | Gamepad                          |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Move        | <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | D-pad or left stick              |
| Jump        | <kbd>W</kbd>, <kbd>↑</kbd>, or <kbd>Space</kbd> | D-pad up or stick up             |
| Punch       | <kbd>J</kbd> or <kbd>Z</kbd>                   | X / □ or A / ✕                   |
| Block       | <kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>X</kbd> (hold)            | RB / R1 or RT / R2 (hold)        |
| Crouch      | <kbd>S</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> (hold)            | D-pad down or stick down (hold)  |
| Special     | <kbd>I</kbd> or <kbd>C</kbd> (when ★ is ready) | Y / △                            |
| Show controls | <kbd>Esc</kbd>                               | Start                            |
| Restart / rematch | <kbd>R</kbd>                             | Back / Select                    |

Two layouts share the keyboard: **WASD** for the left hand with <kbd>J</kbd> punch, <kbd>K</kbd> block, <kbd>I</kbd> special for the right; or the **arrow keys** in the right hand with <kbd>Z</kbd> punch, <kbd>X</kbd> block, <kbd>C</kbd> special for the left.

Keys are bound by position, so on AZERTY, Dvorak, and other layouts, use the keys where those letters sit on a US QWERTY keyboard.

<kbd>Esc</kbd> or the **Controls** button in the bottom-right corner shows this list in the game.

## Moves

### Punch and air kick

Punch on the ground for a quick jab. Punch in the air for a kick that hits crouching opponents too. A punch pressed up to 4 frames early, while you're still recovering or stunned, comes out on the first frame you can act, so mashing isn't needed.

### Jump

Hold jump to keep jumping. Press a direction during the takeoff to jump that way. You can jump over a crouching or sweeping opponent to switch sides.

### Block

Hold block to guard against hits from the front. You stand still while guarding, and letting go lets you act again immediately. A blocked hit holds your guard for 0.2 seconds.

### Crouch

Hold crouch to duck: punches pass over you, but air kicks still hit. You stand still while crouching, and block wins if you hold both.

### Sweep

Punch while crouching for a low spinning kick that knocks the opponent off their feet (10 damage). They stay down about a second, can't be hit while down, then get back up. Blocking and crouching don't stop a sweep; only jumping does, and the jump has to start before the kick comes out.

### Special throw

Land **3 hits in a row** (punches, air kicks, or sweeps that aren't blocked) without getting hit, and a **★** pulses at the inner end of your health bar. Getting hit before you use it loses it.

Press special on the ground to grab: if the opponent is within reach, you lift them, flip them over your head, and slam them down behind you for **20 damage**, switching sides. Blocking and crouching don't stop it; a jumping or stunned opponent can't be grabbed. A missed grab still uses up the star. Only players get the special; the CPU never does.

## The CPU

The computer walks in and jabs or sweeps, jumps in with air kicks, backs off, and sometimes blocks or ducks your punches, answering a ducked punch with a sweep.

Too hard? Press <kbd>F1</kbd> in a match to switch it to **easy**: it's too slow to block punches and defends and attacks less. "CPU difficulty: easy" shows in the bottom-left corner while it's on, and the setting carries over restarts. Press <kbd>F1</kbd> again for the default.
